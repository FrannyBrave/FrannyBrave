# Hi, I'm Francesca Brave 👋

### Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst | Threat Research | OSINT | Adversary & Infrastructure Analysis

Cybersecurity professional focused on **cyber threat intelligence, threat research, OSINT/WEBINT, threat hunting, adversary behavior, vulnerability intelligence, and technical investigations**.

My work centers on turning fragmented technical and open-source signals into structured, defensible intelligence about **threat actors, campaigns, infrastructure, indicators of compromise, vulnerabilities, TTPs, and potential attack paths**.

I enjoy the investigative side of cybersecurity — starting with an intelligence requirement, suspicious activity, or technical indicator and working through the evidence to understand **what happened, how the activity operates, why it matters, and what action should follow**.
